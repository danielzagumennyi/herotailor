Superheroes save the city.

Hero Tailor is a cozy sewing game for Android. Run a small tailor shop for superheroes: repair burnt and torn suits or sew brand-new ones from scratch. Cut the pattern, stitch the seams on your sewing machine and fit the suit on a mannequin.

In Hero Tailor you run a small atelier for superheroes: repair damaged suits or sew new ones from scratch — cut the pattern, sew the seams and fit the suit on a mannequin — for a whole team of heroes. A relaxing, tactile craft game for phones, with a twist: not every client is a hero.
