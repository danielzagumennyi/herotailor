Hero Tailor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026
In short: Hero Tailor is a single-player game. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account, and it does not show ads. Your progress is saved on your device, and the game does not collect or share any data.

This policy explains how Hero Tailor (the “Game”), developed by Unnoised Games (“we”, “us”), handles information. Contact: unnoised.games@gmail.com.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game does not collect or share any personal or device data: no name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, messages, device identifiers, analytics or crash reports. The Game has no user accounts, chat or social features.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. Google Play

Downloads, pre-registration, updates and any purchases are handled by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy. If the Game offers in-app purchases, payments are processed by Google; we never receive your payment card details.

4. Advertising

The current version of the Game shows no ads and does not use the Android advertising ID. If this changes, we will update this policy and the Game’s store listing before the change takes effect.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the Game. For any question about this policy, write to unnoised.games@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
